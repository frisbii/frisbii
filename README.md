hello! i'm **madi**, an undergraduate student majoring in computer science and architectural studies at amherst college (go mammoths! 🦣)

notable projects:
* [*CGC*](https://github.com/jesselooney/cgc) [(report)](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1YgQJzbYpk5JctnAHgmZ6_eMJ-EtO7SkC/view?usp=sharing) is a research tool that implements polymorphic garbage collection for C. Developed for an independent study at Amherst College. Key technologies: **C**, **Python**.
* [*A-Normalized Call-by-Push-Value*](https://github.com/yiyunliu/cbpv-anf) [(slides)](https://drive.google.com/file/d/18yWpVypF1sntZ9H451IVGQ20QEjaFhdR/view?usp=sharing) is a language that combines the explicit sequencing and optimizations of A-normalized form with the adaptability of call-by-push-value. Contributed to during a research internship at the University of Pennsylvania. Key technologies: **Rocq**, *formal methods*
* [*CONFER*](https://github.com/frisbii/CONFER) [(abstract)](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1sqpdErdmmW0VwVNvuCVQr-cgcFcCo1Hl/view?usp=sharing) is a research toolchain for rapidly evaluating and comparing the hardware costs among number formats. Developed during a research fellowship at Amherst College. Key technologies: **Python**, **Vivado**, **FPGAs**.
* [*breathing room*](https://github.com/hex-key/breathing-room) is a point-and-click visual novel, reliving the complex emotional experience of moving to college for the first time. Developed for *HackUMass XI*. Key technologies: **Python**, **Pygame**.
* [*Votable*](https://github.com/frisbii/Votable) is a voting dashboard app giving community members the tools needed to educate and advocate in local elections. Developed for *Ready, Set, App! 2023*; won second in the state of North Carolina. Key technologies: **Javascript**, **React Native**.
* [*Study Corner*](https://github.com/frisbii/study-corner) is a cozy productivity application which encourages and rewards healthy work habits. Developed for COSC-112 at Amherst College. Key technologies: **Java**, **Swing**.
